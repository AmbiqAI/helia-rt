Search the HELIA adapter inventory by operator, family or **input data type**. Each result lists important constraints and links to the adapter at this documentation's source revision.

A listed type can use an optimized, reference or storage-only path. Shape, weight/output types and build settings still determine whether your model is supported.

<details>
<summary>How to read type support</summary>

- **FP16 arithmetic** needs the matching heliaCORE feature and target support. Storage operations such as `DEQUANTIZE` are different.
- **int4 weights** appear in compute-operator notes. They do not imply int4 activations.
- **Fallback is operator-specific.** Check both allocation and invocation status. See [Model compatibility](/helia-rt/guide/model-compatibility/) and [Floating point](/helia-rt/guide/floating-point/).
- Operators outside this inventory may come from upstream Reference sources. Registering an operator does not prove that all its types and options are supported.

</details>
